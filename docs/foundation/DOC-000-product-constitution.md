# DOC-000 - Product Constitution

Version: 1.0 Draft
Status: Draft
Owner: Product / Enterprise Architecture
Domain: Foundation

## Purpose

This document defines the highest-level product principles for Erstatningshjaelp.

It guides future architecture, design, AI, business and implementation decisions.

## Mission

Erstatningshjaelp helps users understand whether their situation may be relevant for further review through a safe, respectful and responsible digital process.

## Vision

The product should be a trusted digital first conversation for case intake.

It combines:

- digital first conversation
- responsible AI assistance
- structured business decisions
- respectful routing
- professional case handling

## North Star

Every user should leave Erstatningshjaelp with more clarity, more trust and a clearer understanding of the next step than when they began.

## Product Promise

The product does not promise outcomes.

It promises to:

- listen carefully
- explain clearly
- guide respectfully
- be honest about uncertainty
- help the user understand what may happen next

## Core Values

- Trust before efficiency
- Human before technology
- Explain before asking
- No false promises
- Respect uncertainty
- One step at a time
- Leave the user better than we found them

## Golden Principles

- Speak Human, Store Structured
- Understanding Before Questioning
- Confirm Before Classify
- Never Ask Twice
- Explain Why
- One Thought Per Screen
- Progressive Disclosure
- Smart Skip
- Respect Emotional Context
- Responsible Acceptance
- Leave the User Better Than We Found Them
- Policy Before Code
- AI Assists, Humans Decide
- GitHub is the Single Source of Truth

## Decision Hierarchy

When two goals conflict, use this order:

1. Responsibility
2. User trust
3. Data quality
4. Business value
5. Efficiency

## Definition of Success

The product succeeds when:

- the user feels understood
- the user understands the next step
- the business accepts the right cases
- complex cases are not routed too early without review
- internal users receive a clear decision basis
- AI-supported decisions are explainable
- the platform can be audited and improved over time

## Definition of Frozen

An architecture area may only be marked Frozen when:

- documentation exists
- GitHub is updated
- related ADRs exist where needed
- backlog is updated
- handover is updated
- implementation can begin without ambiguity

## Governance Rule

No major product, AI, business-rule or architecture change should be implemented without:

- architecture reference
- policy reference where relevant
- decision reference where relevant
- acceptance criteria
- test requirements
- ADR if it changes an accepted principle
