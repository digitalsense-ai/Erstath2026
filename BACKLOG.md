# Product Backlog

This file is the single parking lot for new product ideas that are outside the current delivery scope.

## Working rule

- Finish the agreed end-to-end case journey before adding new scope.
- New ideas may be discussed, but they are added here instead of being built immediately.
- Backlog items are reviewed only when the current milestone is complete.
- Items in this file are not commitments, priorities, or approved requirements.

## Later ideas

### Administration and leadership
- Management dashboard with case volume, conversion, workload, response time, SLA, bottlenecks, and financial overview.

### Advanced intelligence
- Expanded document extraction across all document types.
- Automated inconsistency detection across conversations, documents, and authority correspondence.
- Advanced decision support and case-quality scoring.
- Automated draft generation for authority submissions and responses.

### Operations
- Team calendar and workload planning.
- Advanced employee performance and capacity reporting.
- Configurable reminder and escalation rules.

### Customer experience
- Optional progress percentage across the full case journey.
- Additional notification channels and preference controls.
- Extended support for reopening and appeal journeys beyond the MVP flow.

## Completed or in current scope

The following belong to the current end-to-end journey and must be completed before this backlog is reviewed:

- Customer screening and guided interview
- Cooperation, permissions, consent, and power of attorney
- Case creation and document collection
- Employee case inbox and case handling
- Submission and ongoing authority dialogue
- Customer tasks, messages, and status
- Case decision and closure
- Traceable case summary, findings, and timeline
